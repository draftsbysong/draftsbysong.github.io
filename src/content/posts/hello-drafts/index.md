Most ideas worth writing down are rarely finished on the first pass. Treating a blog as a collection of working drafts lowers the friction to publish, revise, and discard weak assumptions when better models or counterexamples appear.

## Conventions

Each entry carries one of three stage markers:

- `note` — short observations, references, or single-idea breakdowns.
- `draft` — longer arguments still under active revision.
- `essay` — pieces that have stabilized after iteration.

## Supported Features

Markdown and MDX files live under `src/content/posts/<slug>/index.md`, so each article can keep its local figures and supporting notes alongside the text.

Inline and block equations render via KaTeX, for example:

$$
P(H \mid E) = \frac{P(E \mid H)\,P(H)}{P(E)}
$$
